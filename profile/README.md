<h1 align="center">BricksChat</h1>
<p align="center"><strong>Describe it. Build it.</strong></p>

Meet **Bricky**, your AI building companion. Turn an idea, a sketch or a photo into an original brick build, then bring it to life with step-by-step building instructions.

### From imagination to bricks

- **Start with an idea.** Describe what you want to build, or share a picture or drawing.
- **Build with your collection.** Tell Bricky which parts you have.
- **Make it real.** Explore the build and follow the building instructions.

BricksChat is a project from [Mereology Labs](https://mereologylabs.com), exploring how AI can turn ideas into buildable assemblies of real parts.
